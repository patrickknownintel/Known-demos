2022
Initial close
March 2022 — Deal closed by Tom Sato. Brett Collins (VP Operations at the time) served as the internal champion and drove the evaluation. Initial ACV $62K. Enterprise + Analytics package. CFO approved after a standard procurement review — no friction at close.
The relationship started clean. Brett was highly engaged, responsive, and had a clear mandate to modernise the operations tech stack. Tom built a strong rapport with Brett over the evaluation period — that relationship became the foundation the account relied on for the next three years.
Clean close Rep: Tom Sato Champion: Brett Collins
2023
Implementation failure · sensitivity created
Q3 2023 — Implementation delayed 6 weeks due to environment mismatch. The rollout stalled when a configuration incompatibility with Inline's existing data infrastructure wasn't caught in pre-launch QA. The issue escalated to VP level on both sides. Tom managed the recovery personally.
Resolved October 2023. Brett accepted the resolution but made a point of it: "We can't go through that again." That comment has been referenced in two subsequent meetings. This account is measurably more sensitive to deployment risk than comparable accounts in the book. Any future product changes, integrations, or expansion work must acknowledge this history.
Institutional memory flag: Inline team will reference the 2023 rollout when evaluating any new deployment. Do not minimise it. Acknowledge it directly and show what has changed.
Deployment sensitivity created Rep: Tom Sato
2024
Renewal · CFO friction · APAC first raised
July 2024 — Renewal closed at $62K. Tom Sato and Brett Collins drove it together. Flat renewal — no expansion, no price increase agreed at the time. Brett pushed for stability given the 2023 implementation memory.
Late 2024 — CFO pricing friction. An 8% price increase was proposed mid-cycle without advance notice to the CFO (Mark Tessaro). The CFO flagged this directly — required a 2-week hold before signature. Brett apologised on Inline's behalf and absorbed the increase. Tom handled the recovery. The CFO's name never appeared in a prior touchpoint — he came in cold and pushed back immediately.
Q4 2024 — APAC expansion first raised. Brett and Tom discussed a potential APAC expansion during a Q4 review. Estimated +$60K ACV. No formal scoping occurred. This was a verbal conversation, not a proposal.
Institutional memory flag: Never introduce commercial terms or pricing changes to Inline before the CFO is in the conversation. Late CFO involvement created a 2-week hold and nearly derailed the renewal.
CFO friction — pricing surprise Rep: Tom Sato Champion: Brett Collins
2025
Rep transition × 2 · support escalation begins
June 2025 — Tom Sato leaves. Dana Kim inherits the account. No formal handoff was captured. Dana was brought up to speed informally — what she knew came from Tom verbally, not from any system. The three-year relationship Tom had built with Brett was not transferred.
Q3–Q4 2025 — Support escalation opens. Analytics module bug averaging 2 incidents per month. Sarah Chen (Director IT) becomes the primary day-to-day contact. Dana manages through the escalation.
Q4 2025 — APAC expansion revisited. APAC comes up again in Q4 review. No scoping call occurs. Brett is still engaged but beginning to signal his own potential departure.
December 2025 — Dana Kim transitions. Marcus Webb inherits. Again, no formal handoff. Marcus starts with limited prior context — the account is mid-escalation, the champion is signalling departure, and the APAC conversation is unresolved.
Transition note: This account has had three reps in three years. No warm handoff was completed at either transition. Each incoming rep started without the relationship context of the previous rep. The 2023 implementation sensitivity, the CFO pricing history, and Brett's relationship depth with Tom were all at risk of being lost.
